The bike hunt

DCT only, frunk required, no exceptions on either
  • The bike: 2014+ Honda NC700X DCT or NC750X DCT2015 NC700X preferred. Dual-clutch automatic is a hard requirement, and the NC's built-in frunk storage is a deciding feature. The bike must not be able to stall at stops and must sit in 1st gear at a complete stop with no clutch lever held, hands fully off the bars.
  • Backup: Honda CBR650R E-Clutch (2024+)The only US CBR with E-Clutch, and an absolute backup only, for the right price. E-Clutch confirmation required. A CTX700/CTX700N DCT counts only with about 20L or more of frunk-equivalent storage (saddlebags and/or top case). Scooters are out.
  • The moneyPurchase budget $2,000 to $5,000. Asking-price ceiling $6,500 on all sources for the NC/CTX, dealers included (negotiation applies to dealers too); $8,500 for the CBR650R E-Clutch. Below-market asks get flagged: the goal is to pay below market.
  • The territoryFacebook Marketplace, Craigslist, and private sellers within 70 miles of Dublin, CA. Official dealership listings are in scope anywhere in Northern California, Fresno northward.
